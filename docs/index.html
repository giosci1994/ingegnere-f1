F1 25 · UDP telemetry · Local voice

Your race engineer, on the radio.

A home-made race engineer for EA SPORTS F1 25 (2026 Season Pack). It reads the game's UDP telemetry, decides what is worth saying and says it in Italian, with a team-radio effect, on a web app for your phone. Everything runs in one Docker container at home.

// what it does

Talks when it matters, stays quiet otherwise

One phrase at a time, by priority, no overlaps. Three verbosity levels and a switch for every group of calls.

📡

Live calls

Everything the telemetry knows, said at the right moment.

  • Flags, safety car, VSC, penalties
  • Tyres, fuel, battery, damage, weather
  • Gaps, positions, laps to go, lap times
🧮

Strategy

Pit loss measured on each track, degradation from your clean laps.

  • Undercut and overcut calls
  • Where you rejoin after a stop
  • Estimates are said as estimates
🏁

Braking and exits

Compared with your best lap, one short call on the next straight.

  • Braking point, minimum speed, throttle
  • Only when you lose time and there is a cause
📈

Racing line

Every lap stored as a compact trace. Overlay any two laps of a track, from any session or driver.

🔧

Session summary

Laps, sectors, ideal lap, stops, errors, and setup hints, each backed by the data point behind it.

🪽

2026 rules

UDP format 2026 (24 cars, active aero, Overtake Mode), with 2025 as a fallback.

// how it works

Console, home server, phone

The console audio is left alone: the engineer talks from your phone.

1

The game sends

F1 25 sends UDP telemetry at 20 Hz to the server, port 20777.

2

The engineer decides

Rules run five times a second; the radio queue picks one phrase by priority, and Piper speaks it locally.

3

Your phone plays

Open the page, tap «Collega la radio» once, keep it in the foreground. Android and iPhone.

// install

One container, on any amd64 box

Docker and Docker Compose. The two Italian Piper voices are downloaded separately (about 90 MB).

git clone https://github.com/giosci1994/ingegnere-f1.git
cd ingegnere-f1
# put the riccardo (x_low) and paola (medium) Piper voices in ./voci
docker compose up -d --build

In the game (Settings → Telemetry): UDP on, broadcast off, the server's IP address, port 20777, 20 Hz, UDP format 2026. Then open http://<server-ip>:8777 on the phone.

// privacy

Nothing leaves your home

🏠

Local only

No cloud, no accounts, no analytics. The voice is synthesized on your server; no cloned voices, no broadcast audio.

🔕

Telemetry off

onnxruntime's usage telemetry is switched off in the image and in the code.

🔒

Home network

There is no login: keep ports 8777 and 20777 on your home network, never on the internet.
